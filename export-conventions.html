Archive Export Conventions (REF-1013-F)

This page (REF-1013-F) describes how documents leave the archive system, and how what comes back is reconciled afterwards.

Two-part export

An export leaves the system in two parts. The first part is the body description, a short account of what the document contains. The second part is the acknowledgement line, which is the entry the system reads when it closes a record.

What the acknowledgement line carries

The acknowledgement line carries the document number, a forward slash, and the current version. Those three together identify the record that a body description belongs to.

Reconciliation

The body description is logged first. The acknowledgement line follows as a separate entry, and the two are paired afterwards.

A body description that arrives on its own is logged as unmatched. Unmatched records stay open until the next review cycle, and a record still open after two cycles is flagged for manual review.

The field naming here is the same convention the earlier notes use, and it is unchanged.
